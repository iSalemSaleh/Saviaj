# Refund Policy for Saviaj

*Last updated: 2026*

Saviaj is operated by **Sibranet Technologies Ltd** and acts as a digital marketplace connecting Riders and Drivers.

## 1. Platform Role

Saviaj does not provide transport services directly. All services are provided by independent Drivers.

## 2. Payment Processing

Payments are processed via Stripe. Users agree to Stripe's terms.

## 3. Refund Eligibility

Refunds may be issued if:

- Trip cancelled before it begins
- Payment made but no service delivered
- Duplicate or accidental charges
- Verified disputes

## 4. Non-Refundable Situations

No refunds if:

- Trip completed
- Rider fails to attend
- Service delivered as agreed

## 5. Dispute Resolution

Contact: [info@sibranet.co.uk](mailto:info@sibranet.co.uk)

## 6. Processing Refunds

Refunds processed via original payment method.

## 7. Chargebacks

Abuse may result in suspension.
