# Privacy Policy for Saviaj

*Last updated: 2026*

Saviaj is operated by **Sibranet Technologies Ltd** ("we", "us", "our") and functions as a digital mobility marketplace connecting Riders, Casual Drivers, Commercial Drivers ("Pro Drivers"), and Business Drivers/Fleet Operators. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your personal data when you use the Saviaj application and services.

## 1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to Riders, Casual Drivers, Commercial Drivers, Business Drivers/Fleet Operators, and visitors to the Saviaj platform.

## 2. Information we collect

We may collect:

- Account information (name, email, phone number, user role)
- Business information (business name, assigned drivers, fleet data where applicable)
- Trip and route information
- Location data while the app is in use
- Payment information via Stripe (we do not store full card details)
- Communications between users
- Technical data such as IP address, device type, and logs

## 3. How we use your information

We use your information to:

- Provide and operate the platform
- Match riders and drivers
- Enable price negotiation and trip agreements
- Process payments
- Support fleet management and driver assignment
- Improve and secure the service
- Prevent fraud and misuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## 4. Legal basis

We process data under:

- Contract necessity
- Legal obligations
- Legitimate interests
- User consent where applicable

## 5. Sharing information

We may share information with:

- Other users (for trip coordination)
- Stripe and payment processors
- Hosting and infrastructure providers
- Legal authorities if required

## 6. Data retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary to provide the service and meet legal obligations. Users may request deletion of their data.

## 7. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, and request portability of your data, and to object to or restrict processing.

## 8. Children

Users must be 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

## 9. Data security procedures

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Secure authentication
- Restricted access to systems

However, no system is completely secure and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

## 10. Policy updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Significant changes will be communicated via the app or website. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
